Confirm available print areas on selected blanks, accounting for seams, curves, handles, notebook binding and hinges. Use the blue and white logo masters for alternate blank colors. White underbase may be needed on blue products. The hex value records the digital brand reference, not a confirmed Pantone or CMYK ink formula; request physical color matching and material proofs. Printer must set separations, trapping and underbase. Proposed inset logos require no bleed; any all-over coaster print needs its supplier's bleed template.
